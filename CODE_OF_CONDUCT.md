# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful and free of harassment for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone starts somewhere.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Be honest about uncertainty. In quantitative work, saying "I don't know if this holds out of sample" is a strength.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Promoting trading products, signals, or investment schemes in project spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on their GitHub profile ([@Walid-Soufyane](https://github.com/Walid-Soufyane)). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone is officially representing the project in public.
